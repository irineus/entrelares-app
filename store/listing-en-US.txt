=== APP NAME (<= 30 chars) ===
Entrelares: Co-Parenting

=== SHORT DESCRIPTION (<= 80 chars) ===
Co-parenting calendar: who has the kids today, the next handover, every swap.

=== FULL DESCRIPTION (<= 4000 chars) ===
Two homes, one childhood. Entrelares organizes everyday life for separated parents who share custody: one shared calendar shows who has the kids today, who takes over next, and the handover time for each day.

WHO HAS THE KIDS TODAY
Open the app and the answer is on the first screen. The month calendar colors each caregiver's days, and shows the handover time whenever the kids change homes.

DAY SWAPS, ASKED AND ANSWERED IN THE APP
Need to trade a day? Propose the swap with a short message ("doctor's appointment at 2pm") — nothing moves on the calendar until the other caregiver answers. Whoever answers can reply with a note, and all of it is recorded. If nobody answers in time, the request is approved automatically — and a reminder comes first, with the exact day and time.

NOTICES AND DAY ACCOUNTS
Something came up today? Send the other caregiver a notice ("running 15 minutes late"). Once the day has passed, write a day account — a short record of what happened that day. It stays attached to that day and is never edited: a correction becomes a new account, and both are kept.

A HISTORY NO FAMILY MEMBER CAN REWRITE
Every calendar change is recorded: who asked, who approved, and the messages they exchanged. No family member can edit or delete the history. On the Premium plan, export a PDF report of any period with the full timeline, verifiable by QR code.

PERIOD SUMMARY
See how many days each caregiver had, planned and actual; how many swaps each one gave up and received; and the projected balance, including the future swaps already agreed on.

EACH CHILD'S AGENDA
Leave notes on a day for the other home. On Premium, each child's full agenda: school, health, medicine and activities, with routines and reminders.

EXPENSES AND CHAT
On Premium, shared expenses, with each payment confirmed by whoever receives it, and the family chat: permanent, with read receipts.

GRANDPARENTS AND OTHER CAREGIVERS
Grandparents, a nanny or another caregiver can join the family with their own role, invited by email by the family's admins; caregivers beyond the free plan's limit join with Premium. And a Viewer follows along without taking part: sees the calendar, the agenda and the reports, and changes nothing.

ALWAYS UP TO DATE
With the app open, the calendar updates in real time: requests, approvals and changes appear without a reload. With the app closed, requests, approvals, notices and reminders arrive as phone notifications.
